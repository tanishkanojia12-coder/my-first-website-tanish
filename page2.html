TEENAGE INTRODUCTION

My Educational Journey After High School

After completing my high school education in 2023, I decided to change my educational board from IGCSE to the HSC Maharashtra State Board.

I took admission in Malini Kishor Sanghvi College of Commerce and Economics for my Junior College education (11th and 12th standard). I chose the Commerce stream with Mathematics.

My 11th Standard Experience

My 11th standard was a period of adjustment as I was getting familiar with a new board and different subjects. I considered myself an average student during this year and scored 61% overall.

This year helped me understand the importance of consistency, time management and regular studies.

My 12th Standard Experience

My 12th standard was the HSC Board Examination year 2024-2025. The beginning of 12th standard was difficult for me because I initially found the subjects and teaching methods challenging.

However, I decided to work hard and improve my academic performance. I dedicated more time to my studies and focused on understanding the subjects.

All my hard work paid off when I scored 81% in my 12th HSC Board Examination. This became the best academic year of my life and gave me a lot of confidence.

My MAH CET Examination

In 2025, I appeared for the MAH CET examination for admission to BMS and BBA courses.

I scored an impressive 89 percentile in the examination. This was another important milestone in my educational journey.

My Gap Year

After appearing for MAH CET, I decided to take a gap year. This period became an opportunity for me to think about my future, understand my interests and plan my next academic steps.

I believe that every stage of life teaches us something new. My teenage years have helped me understand the value of hard work, patience, determination and self-improvement.
